# Volunteer onboarding and safeguarding

Every volunteer completes onboarding before their first shift. Onboarding has four steps:

1. A signed volunteer agreement and a photo ID check.
2. Two references, at least one from outside the volunteer's family.
3. A safeguarding briefing, completed within 14 days of the agreement.
4. A short data-protection briefing covering what volunteers may and may not access.

## Roles with children

Volunteers who work with children also need a police clearance certificate issued within the
last 12 months. The certificate is renewed every three years, and the safeguarding briefing
is repeated annually.

## Scheduling and absence

Shifts are published ten days in advance. A volunteer who cannot attend should cancel at
least 24 hours before the shift so the coordinator can find cover. Three unexplained
absences in a quarter trigger a conversation with the volunteer coordinator; they are not
an automatic dismissal.

## Expenses

Volunteers may claim pre-approved travel at the same rate as staff. Claims follow the staff
expense process and need the coordinator's approval before submission.
