# Records retention policy

Amanah Foundation keeps records only as long as they are needed for audit, legal or
operational reasons. Retention starts from the end of the calendar year in which the record
was created.

## Financial records

Ledger entries, bank statements, receipts and grant agreements are kept for seven years.
Supporting documents for a restricted grant are kept for seven years after the grant closes.

## Uploaded documents

Files uploaded to the document assistant are retained for 24 months from the upload date.
After 24 months the file and its derived chunks are purged from the assistant. The purge job
runs on the first working day of each month and is recorded in the audit log.

## Personnel records

Personnel files are kept for the duration of employment plus 24 months. Safeguarding
records are the exception: they are kept for seven years after the case is closed.

## Deletion requests

A data subject may ask for deletion at privacy@amanah.example. The privacy team confirms the
request within 30 days. Records under a legal hold cannot be deleted until the hold is lifted;
the requester is told which record is held and why.
