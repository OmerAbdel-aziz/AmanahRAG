# Refunds and expense approvals

## Refunds to donors

A donor may ask for a refund within 14 days of a donation. The request must include the
receipt number and the original payment method. Refunds are returned to the same payment
method; cash refunds are not allowed.

- Up to AED 5,000: approved by the finance officer.
- Above AED 5,000: approved by the finance director.
- Above AED 50,000: approved by the finance director and the board treasurer.

Refunds requested after 14 days are treated as exceptions and require the finance director
regardless of amount.

## Staff expenses

Expense claims are submitted within 30 days of the spend. Receipts are mandatory above
AED 100. Mileage is reimbursed at AED 0.75 per kilometre for approved travel only.

Claims are paid in the next payroll cycle after approval. A claim without a receipt above
the threshold is returned to the submitter rather than rejected.
